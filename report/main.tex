\subsection{Daily Ticker Sentiment (Sanity Check)}
We aggregate headline--level FinBERT scores to per\-ticker, per\-day sentiment and observe meaningful variation outside of earnings dates. In our 2022--2024 sample, we obtain 39 ticker\-days across 7 tickers (42 headlines total). The composite sentiment (pos\,\textminus\,neg) shows broad dispersion (overall mean $-0.10$, std $0.65$, interquartile range $[-0.74,\,0.26]$), indicating the signal is not flat and can serve as a foundation for features.
